# Larder customer call: Ana

Wed, Oct 21, 2026 · 2:00 PM · 38 min
Attendees: Sam Ortiz, Ana Ferreira

### Background
- Ana is 72 and lives on her own
- Her daughter, Carla, lives across town and does most of Ana's grocery shopping
- Carla set up Larder for both of them about eight months ago

### Current workflow
- Ana adds items to a shared list during the week, mostly by voice dictation
- Carla shops for Ana on her way over, usually once or twice a week
- Before shopping, Carla visits and takes a photo of Ana's fridge to check what's actually there
- Ana sometimes adds items that aren't groceries (reminders, notes to Carla)

### Observations
- The list serves as an ongoing line of communication between Ana and Carla
- Ana sometimes adds items she knows Carla will see first thing in the morning
- Ana is comfortable with the app but prefers large text; uses iPhone accessibility settings
- Carla's fridge photos are stored in her camera roll, not in Larder

### Pain points
- Text size inside the app doesn't always follow her phone settings
- Dictation sometimes produces odd items that Carla has to interpret
- Not sure when Carla has seen an item

### Feature feedback
- Would like to know when Carla has "read" the list
- Liked the idea of seeing the fridge photo in the app, but didn't ask for it directly
- Has not used recipe import

### Follow-ups
- Sam to check Dynamic Type support on the item detail screen
- Send gift card (Ana asked for it to go to Carla's email)
