### Background
- Rob lives with his partner; has used Larder for over two years
- Self-described "power user"; uses Larder for groceries, hardware store runs, and a weekly meal-prep list
- Annual subscriber

### Current workflow
- Maintains several short-lived lists, one per trip, created from a template he keeps in Notes
- Items are written as name and quantity only ("Eggs, 12")
- Checks everything off in-store and lets completed lists archive automatically
- Partner occasionally adds items; Rob cleans them up before shopping

- Wants faster entry: bulk paste, keyboard shortcuts on iPad, quantity as a separate field
- Finds the app getting "chattier" with recent updates

### Feature feedback

- Asked for a "pro mode": dense layout, quantity column, aisle sorting, no avatars
- Tried recipe import; found it useful once but said the parsed quantities were wrong
- Not interested in item comments or photos

### Follow-ups
- Sam to share pro mode feedback with the team
- Rob offered to beta test anything related to bulk entry
