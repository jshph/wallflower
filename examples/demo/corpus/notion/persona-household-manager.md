# Persona: The Household Manager

**Owner:** Sam Ortiz · **Last edited:** Nov 18, 2025 · **Status:** Final
**Research basis:** 14 interviews (Aug–Sept 2025), 2 diary studies, Q2 2025 signup survey (n=388)

---

## Meet Morgan, the Household Manager

Morgan keeps the house running. They plan the week's meals on Sunday, own the list, and delegate the run.

Morgan is 38 and lives with a partner and two kids, 9 and 12. They work full time, mostly from home two days a week. Morgan isn't a "foodie," but they care that the family eats reasonably well and they hate wasting food.

> "If I don't write it down, nobody in this house is going to remember it. So I write it down."

## A typical week

- **Sunday afternoon.** Morgan looks at the week's calendar, picks four or five dinners, and builds the list. Often working from two or three saved recipes.
- **Midweek.** Other people in the household add things as they run out, usually without much detail ("milk"). Morgan tidies these up.
- **The run.** Morgan's partner does the main shop on the way home from work on Mondays. Morgan sometimes does a second, smaller trip.
- **After.** Morgan checks the list to see what got bought, and follows up on anything that didn't.

## Goals

- Spend less time thinking about groceries
- Make sure the person doing the shop gets the right things without a phone call from the aisle
- Waste less food
- Feel like the household runs without them holding every detail in their head

## Frustrations

- "I have to re-type ingredients from recipes every single week."
- Other people add vague items, then Morgan gets the wrong thing
- The person shopping texts photos from the store to ask which one
- Lists from other apps get messy and Morgan ends up starting over

## What Morgan needs from Larder

1. **Fast planning.** Getting a week of meals onto the list in minutes.
2. **Control.** Morgan is the owner. Others contribute, but Morgan decides what the list looks like.
3. **Clean hand-off.** The shopper sees a clear, finished list and doesn't have to interpret anything.
4. **Closure.** When the run is done, it's done.

## Secondary roles in Morgan's household

- **The Contributor** (partner, kids): adds items occasionally, rarely opens the app otherwise.
- **The Runner** (often the partner): does the shop from the list. Wants the list to be unambiguous. Opens the app in the store and nowhere else.

## Design principles that come from Morgan

- Optimize for the planner's Sunday session first
- The list is a plan, then a checklist, then done
- Every list has an owner
- Don't make the Runner think
