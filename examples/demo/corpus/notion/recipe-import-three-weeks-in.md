# Recipe import, three weeks in

**Author:** Maya · **Oct 24** · For Thursday planning
**Related:** Q4: Recipe import · LAR-110 · Funnel dashboard

---

Import has been out to everyone (the + menu version) since Sept 30. Onboarding entry point isn't out yet (LAR-114, now Cycle 9), so this is the "people who went looking for it" number, not the "we put it in front of them" number. Writing it down now so Thursday isn't based on vibes.

## Usage

3.1% of weekly actives have used it at least once.

- 0.8% have used it more than once
- 94% parse success on the top 50 sites, 71% overall (long tail is bad, as expected)
- Median items added per import: 11. Median items removed right after: 4 (mostly staples. LAR-116 should help)
- Top sources: NYT Cooking, Allrecipes, a handful of creators on Instagram

Too early to call, but it's not the pull we hoped.

## New households

New households per week are up 3% vs. the Sept baseline (1,840 → ~1,895). Target is +15% by Dec 31. That's within noise, and again, the onboarding piece isn't live, so I'm not reading much into it yet.

Of new households since Sept 30, 2.4% made their first list from an import.

## The thing I didn't expect

Sharing invites are up 22% over the same period, and I can't fully explain why.

- It's not double counting (Dev checked)
- It started the first week of October, before the Android invite fix landed on the 19th
- Onboarding didn't change, so it's not that
- Invites per household are up, not just households sending invites. People are adding a third and fourth member
- Acceptance rate is flat, so these aren't junk invites

Possible explanations: seasonal (people coordinate more heading into the holidays? last year's data is too thin to tell), word of mouth, or something in 3.14 I'm not seeing. I'd rather not guess.

## What I think this means for now

- Recipe import is not failing, it's just not yet the thing pulling people in. The onboarding entry point is the real test, and it's slipped twice.
- We should get LAR-112 and LAR-114 out before we judge the bet.
- I want to understand the invite number before we plan November, even if it doesn't change anything.
