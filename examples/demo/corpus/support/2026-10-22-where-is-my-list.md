Hi Jonah!

So sorry for the scare 💛 Your list is safe, it's in Archive (menu → Archive). When every item on a list gets checked off, Larder moves it there automatically so your home screen stays tidy. Nothing's been deleted, and your roommate will see it come back too.

To bring it back:
1. Tap the menu (☰) in the top left
2. Tap **Archive**
3. Find your list and tap **Restore**

Everything will be right where you left it, including your Thanksgiving stuff. You may want to uncheck the things you need again so they're back at the top.

We're looking at making this less surprising! Thanks for writing in, and I'm really glad it's not gone.
